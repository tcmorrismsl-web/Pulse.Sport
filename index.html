How the excitement number works

A 0–100 read on one question: is this fun to watch right now?

The core

Start with how close the score is, measured against what a normal margin looks like in that sport. A three-run baseball game and a three-point football game are not the same thing. Multiply that by how late it is, so a tie in the first quarter counts for less than a tie with a minute left.

Is anything happening

Closeness alone would rate a scoreless draw a perfect 100. So the number is also scaled by scoring pace against that league's norm. A 0–0 match sits in the 40s; a 3–3 match in the 90s. Same margin, very different afternoon.

What pushes it up

  • Comebacks: how far the margin has moved over the last 15% of the game. A 21–0 run is the single biggest mover.
  • Lead changes: worth about double an ordinary score, then fading over the next few minutes.
  • Upsets: an underdog leading, weighted by the betting line, or by ranking gap when no line exists.
  • Overtime and crunch time in a one-score game.
  • Stakes: the pregame hype score below.
  • Right now: the live situation. In baseball: runners on, outs, the count, and whether the tying or winning run is on base or at the plate. In football: red zone, 4th down and the two-minute drill when the team with the ball is within one score. In soccer: red cards.
  • Fresh scores: a score jolts the number, more for a go-ahead or tying score, then cools off over the next several minutes.
  • Momentum: a team closing the gap or flipping the lead over the last ~12 minutes pushes the number up; a lead that keeps growing pulls it down.

Where it starts

Every game opens at its hype score: rankings, rivalry, and how close the line is. Within the first few minutes of play that anchor hands off to what is actually happening on the field. Preseason games are tagged and get no hype at all.

The zones

  • 0–40 blue: quiet
  • 40–60 green: competitive
  • 60–80 orange: heating up
  • 80–100 red: must-watch

The top end is compressed on purpose. Anything above 90 is a genuinely special game, and 100 is close to unreachable.
